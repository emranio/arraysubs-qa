Reproduced findings

01 · Medium · Box shop entry

The shop button does not open the box modal

Expected: click the box’s shop button and immediately enter the box builder. Actual: the browser opens the product page; the modal stays closed until “Create Subscription Box” is clicked again.

Reproduced with both Build Your Monthly Coffee Box and QPC QA Box.

Reproduction and evidence
  1. Open Shop (Demo box on page 1; QPC box on page 3).
  2. Click the product’s “Create Subscription Box” button.
  3. Observe the product page and a closed modal. Another click is required.

02 · Medium · Box completion

Finishing a box sends the buyer to Cart

Expected: finish selecting the box and go straight to checkout. Actual: the final button says Add to Cart and redirects to /cart-2/. A further “Proceed to Checkout” action is needed.

Reproduced twice with the Demo coffee box and separately with QPC QA Box. This occurs while one-click is enabled for all items; other product types bypass Cart with the same settings.

Reproduction and evidence
  1. Open the Demo coffee box product and click “Create Subscription Box.”
  2. Add one Single-origin Coffee, then click Next.
  3. On Review details, click “Add to Cart.”
  4. Observe Cart with the $22 monthly box, instead of Checkout.

03 · High · Variation purchase blocker

Both published variable products have no selectable options

Member T-shirt shows only “Choose an option” under Size. Membership Tier does the same under Tier. Neither purchase can be completed through the UI.

Scope: this blocks both subscription and non-subscription variation tests. It was reproduced after revisiting the pages and in a separate browser session. The cause has not been attributed to one-click checkout.

Reproduction and admin cross-check
  1. From Shop page 2, click Select options on Member T-shirt or Subscribe Now on Membership Tier.
  2. Open the Size or Tier selector. There are no choices beyond the placeholder.
  3. The grey purchase action cannot complete a valid variation purchase.

Read-only admin inspection confirmed three Size variations for product 6705 (IDs 6706–6708) and two Tier variations for product 6722 (IDs 6723–6724). Attributes are configured for use in variations. These are the only two published variable products; the other variable entry is a draft. No defaults are set.

04 · Medium · Repeating a trial purchase

A second click on the same trial stops at an error

The first click on Library Pass with 14-day Trial reaches checkout correctly. Returning to its product or shop button and clicking again fails while that trial is already in the cart.

The message asks the customer to keep only one trial and reduce quantity to 1, although the existing cart has one trial and the requested quantity is 1. This differs from the one-click replacement behavior that works for regular products.

Reproduction and evidence
  1. Start with an empty cart or another product, and select the trial at quantity 1.
  2. After reaching checkout, return to the trial product page without placing an order.
  3. Click Subscribe Now again at quantity 1.
  4. Observe the trial-limit error and no checkout redirect. Repeating from Shop page 2 also fails.

05 · Medium · Repeating a bundle purchase

A bundle sold individually rejects the repeat click

QPC QA Bundle reaches checkout on its first purchase click. Returning and clicking Subscribe Now again produces “You cannot add another…” instead of returning to checkout with the existing item.

The admin’s “Sold individually” setting is enabled for this product. This is a confirmed restriction/one-click interaction. The Demo Coffee and Tea bundle is a useful counterexample: its repeated click works.

Reproduction and evidence
  1. Add QPC QA Bundle from Shop page 3 or its product page.
  2. Return to the product page, leaving the bundle in the cart.
  3. Click Subscribe Now again. The duplicate-item error blocks the redirect.

Other checks and practical limits

  • Cart replacement passes: a different one-click product removes the earlier item. Mug quantity 2 gives $32; clicking the shop button afterward resets it to 1 and $16.
  • Grouped purchases do not stay together: Coffee Gift Set with Coffee × 1 and Mug × 1 reaches checkout with only Mug. The notice explicitly says Coffee was removed by the last-item-only rule. This matches the current setting’s stated behavior, but is unsuitable if the whole selected group must be purchased together.
  • Required box selections are validated: QPC QA Box refuses Next with no selection and shows “Please select at least 1 item(s) in this step.” Selecting an item allows the builder to continue.
  • Cart links remain: successful purchases show “View cart,” and the cart page stays available, consistent with Disable Cart Page being off.
  • Browser diagnostics: no uncaught JavaScript errors were reported in the inspected sessions. Development/deprecation warnings appeared. This is not a server-log audit.
Coverage boundaries

Guest access is blocked by the site’s Coming soon screen. Tests used the admin account, not an ordinary customer account. The disabled and subscription-only modes, saved custom labels, and Disable Cart Page = on were not switched or tested, honoring the instruction to leave settings unchanged.

No separate purchasable non-subscription box or bundle examples were identified; the available custom types were Subscription Box and Subscription Bundle. The one-time grouped Gift Set was tested separately. Store-credit examples were one-time purchases. Mobile layouts, other themes/templates and completed payments were not tested.
