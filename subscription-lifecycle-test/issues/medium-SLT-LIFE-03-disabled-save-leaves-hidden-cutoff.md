## Reproduction

1. Begin with `skip_renewal.enabled=false` and `skip_renewal.cutoff_days=2`.
2. In ArraySubs Settings → Skip & Pause, enable Skip Renewal, set cutoff to `0`, and save.
3. To restore, enter cutoff `2` while the field is visible.
4. Toggle Enable Skip Renewal off; the cutoff field disappears.
5. Click Save Settings once.
6. Re-read `arraysubs_settings.skip_renewal` from the stored WordPress option.

## Expected result

The single save persists both edited values: `enabled=false` and `cutoff_days=2`.

## Actual result

The UI saved `enabled=false` but left the hidden cutoff at `0`. The restore was incomplete even though the page showed the feature disabled. The cutoff had to be saved while the feature was enabled, followed by a second save to disable it.

- First restore click: `2026-08-07 16:45:12Z`.
