The renewal can be paid again as soon as the gateway reports that the payment failed, also after the subscription moved to another payment method, or, for Stripe and Mollie, at the next reconciliation check if that notification was lost (for Stripe also after such a move). It can also be paid once its settlement window ends, once the PayPal or Paddle subscription is suspended, paused or cancelled, or after an administrator moves a renewal that WooCommerce Stripe put on hold back to Pending payment. Renewals waiting for 3-D Secure, renewals whose payment failed and manual invoices stay payable.
fixed: When Stripe has already taken a renewal's own payment but the order could not be marked paid with it, for example because the order total or the subscription's payment method changed, the customer can no longer pay that renewal again, and it is no longer marked failed when its settlement window ends, so the customer no longer gets "Payment failed" emails for it. A renewal's own Stripe payment refunded in full before the renewal was completed with it no longer completes the renewal later, and a repeated Stripe notice of that refund is no longer recorded as a refund of the payment that later paid the renewal.
