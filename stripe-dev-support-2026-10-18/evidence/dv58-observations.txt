  (WC_Stripe_Intent_Controller::update_order_status_ajax -> process_order_for_confirmed_intent(..., $save_payment_method));
  no token exists while the challenge is open, and the deferred-webhook completion path saves no token.

  ArraySubs StripeSubscriptionCompatibility::validateRenewalActionPaymentMethod() requires a posted WC token equal to
  _gateway_payment_method_id, so without a token there is no usable path on either link.
